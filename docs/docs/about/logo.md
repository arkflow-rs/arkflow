# Logo usage guidelines

![ArkFlow logo](../logo.svg)

The ArkFlow logo is a symbol of the project's identity. These guidelines keep
its use consistent. By using the logo you agree to them.

## TL;DR

- **Allowed** — use it to link to or refer to the ArkFlow project, in
  tutorials/articles/talks, and to show your project is built on ArkFlow.
- **Not allowed** — modifying the logo, using it as *your* primary brand,
  implying official endorsement, putting it on paid merchandise, or using it in
  negative contexts.
- **Ask first** — any commercial use, formal partnership claims, or uses not
  covered here.

## Rights

The ArkFlow name and logo are trademarks of the ArkFlow project team, which
reserves all associated rights. We grant permission to use the marks provided
you follow these guidelines. The permission is non-exclusive and may be revoked.

## Permitted uses

- On your website, blog, or social media, linking to
  https://arkflow-rs.com/.
- In introductions, reviews, tutorials, news, or talks that refer to ArkFlow,
  provided it does not imply official endorsement.
- In non-commercial materials for an open-source project you build on ArkFlow,
  to indicate the association (not as your project's primary logo).
- To showcase your contributions to or use of ArkFlow.
- At community-organized, non-commercial ArkFlow events and meetups.

## Prohibited uses

Unless you have prior written permission:

- **Don't modify the logo** — no changing colors, proportions, fonts, or
  elements; no adding text or graphics. Always use the official files.
- **Don't use it as your own primary brand** identifier for products,
  services, companies, domains, or accounts.
- **Don't imply official endorsement** or sponsorship of your products or
  events.
- **Don't use it on commercial products or paid services** (e.g. merchandise
  for sale).
- **Don't use it in negative, misleading, vulgar, illegal, or controversial**
  contexts.
- **Don't register it** (or similar marks) as your own trademark.

## Visual specifications

- Obtain the latest official files (SVG/PNG) from official ArkFlow channels.
- Use the official colors; ask us about reversed/monochrome variants for
  special backgrounds.
- Keep clear space around the logo.
- Don't stretch, compress, rotate, tilt, or add strokes/shadows/gradients.
